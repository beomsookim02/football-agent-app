Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Football Agent ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the App.

1. Data Storage

Football Agent does not require an account or sign-in. Game progress, career data, and the in-game agent profile are stored locally on your device using the App's local storage.

The current version does not provide cloud save or cross-device sync. Game save data is not uploaded to our servers.

2. Personal Information

Football Agent does not collect Google account information, email addresses, or other account credentials. The App does not require you to create an online account.

3. Analytics and Tracking

The current version does not send gameplay analytics, session data, or game progress to our servers. We do not use personal information for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version does not use Google Sign-In or Supabase for authentication, cloud saves, or gameplay analytics.

5. Advertising

Football Agent does not currently display third-party advertising.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because game data is stored locally on your device, it remains there until it is removed by the App, you reset the relevant career, or the App's local data is deleted. Deleting the App may permanently delete locally stored game progress, depending on the operating system's data handling.

7. Data Security

Local game data is handled by the storage mechanisms provided by your device and operating system. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Football Agent is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from users through an account, cloud-save, or analytics system.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data handling practices change. Any updated version will be posted at this location with a revised "Last Updated" date.
