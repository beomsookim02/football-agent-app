Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Football Agent ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you when you use Football Agent.

1. Information We Collect

Google Account Information

Football Agent allows users to sign in using Google Sign-In. When you choose to sign in with Google, the App may receive basic account information provided through Google's authentication service, such as:

  • A unique user identifier
  • Email address
  • Basic profile information, where provided by Google

Football Agent does not receive or store your Google password.

Game and Cloud Save Data

If you use cloud save features, Football Agent may store information associated with your account, including:

  • Game progress
  • Career and season progress
  • Game-related profile information
  • Other data necessary to provide cloud save and restore functionality

This information may be stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Analytics and Technical Information

Football Agent may collect limited information about how the App is used, such as:

  • App sessions
  • Gameplay progress, including maximum season reached
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers
  • Technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the App

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Football Agent may be used to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Provide cloud save and restore functionality
  • Maintain game progress across supported devices
  • Operate and improve the App
  • Understand general usage patterns
  • Diagnose errors and technical problems
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Football Agent may use third-party services to provide certain features, including:

  • Google, for user authentication
  • Supabase, for authentication-related services, cloud storage, databases, and backend infrastructure

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Football Agent does not currently display third-party advertising.

We do not use personal information for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

If advertising or additional tracking technologies are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as necessary.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain cloud save functionality, comply with applicable legal obligations, or protect the security of the service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Football Agent. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Football Agent is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

8. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may choose not to use Google Sign-In. Certain cloud-based features may not be available without authentication.

You may request access to or deletion of personal information associated with Football Agent by contacting us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its features, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be posted at this location with a revised "Last Updated" date.
